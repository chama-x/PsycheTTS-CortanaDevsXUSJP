The Context

The Challenge

We are building emotional voice interfaces for local, offline devices. While Cloud APIs (Gemini, GPT-4o) offer incredible emotion, they are too slow and too expensive for continuous, private interaction.

For Sinhala, the problem is harder: there are no studio-quality emotional datasets available to train a model from scratch.

Project Goals

  • Latency: Target < 100ms on Edge Hardware.
  • Emotion: Match the "Sadness" or "Joy" of Gemini Pro.
  • Data: Verify a synthetic data collection pipeline.

Synthetic Ground Truth

We have successfully established the Collector phase. By using the Gemini 2.5 Pro API, we are creating a "golden dataset" of Sinhala speech that captures subtle emotional cues (breathiness in sadness, pitch variance in joy).

Why? It's impossible to manually record thousands of emotional lines with consistent quality. Gemini acts as our "Infinite Actor".

Architecture Details

We are actively implementing the following stack to solve the "Transformer Bottleneck" (O(N²) complexity).

Bi-Directional Mamba

Hypothesis: Mamba's State Space Model can capture global emotional context without the quadratic cost of Attention.

Status: In Research Phase

Flow Matching Decoder

Hypothesis: Iterative Flow Matching (Optimal Transport) will yield higher audio fidelity than standard GANs or VITS, with fewer steps than Diffusion.

Status: Implementation Pending
05

Reality Check: Current Status

Concrete Strengths

  • Proven Pipeline Viability: The "Manual Helper" tool successfully captures audio from AI Studio. We have physical .wav files with metadata.
  • Proven Teacher Quality: Gemini 2.5 Pro (Leda voice) successfully generates "sing-song" Sinhala prosody, validating it as a teacher.
  • Proven Infrastructure: Functional Flask Web UI for logging and dataset management is live.

Current Limitations

  • Critical Dataset Size: We currently have < 50 samples. Emotional TTS typically requires 10-20 hours of data to generalize.
  • Blocker Rate Limits: Automated batch generation is blocked by API limits (429 Errors), enforcing manual collection which is slow.
  • Risk Compute Budget: Training the Flow Matching decoder will require GPU resources we haven't yet allocated/benchmarked.
